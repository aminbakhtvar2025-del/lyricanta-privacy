Lyricanta: Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Lyricanta is an app for learning languages with songs and flashcards. It has no ads and no analytics, and it needs no account: everything works without one, except the optional AI features described below. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what the optional account involves and what other services are involved.

Data the app collects

Without an account: none. The app does not collect, send or share personal data.

With an account (optional, only for AI translation of your own lyrics and for "My podcast"): your email address, counters of how much of the monthly AI allowance you used, and the lines you send, which are processed and then discarded. See Account and AI features.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress and settings (cards learned, lessons finished, streak, the languages you chose, audio settings, reminder time). Android's device backup may include them in your own Google account backup, as for any app; you can turn this off in Android settings.
  • Your own lyrics ("My lyrics"). The text you paste or share into the app, and the meanings you add, are stored only in a private database on your phone and are excluded from backups. Only when you use an AI feature are lines sent, once, to be processed (see below).
  • The clipboard is read only when you tap Paste.

Microphone ("Sing along")

When you tap Listen in Sing along, the app asks for the microphone and uses your phone's on-device speech recogniser to follow the song that is playing near you. The sound is processed on your phone only: it is not recorded, not stored and not sent anywhere. Listening stops when you tap Stop or leave the screen. Lines you choose to keep are saved on your phone like your own lyrics.

Language downloads

Each language you learn is downloaded from Google Play when you choose it, as part of the app. Google Play handles these downloads under the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; the app only asks Google Play whether Premium is active on your account and receives no payment details.

Voices

Cards, lessons and Song Stories play recorded audio that comes with the app. If you choose a phone voice instead, or for your own lyrics, the text is passed to your phone's text-to-speech engine, which is governed by its provider's privacy policy.

On-phone translation and AI

"My lyrics" can translate your lines into your language on your phone with Google's ML Kit: the text is not sent anywhere. The first time, the app downloads a language file from Google (about 30 MB). ML Kit may send Google technical usage data such as the device model, the app version and performance figures, as described in the ML Kit terms; it does not include the text you translate.

On phones that offer it, some AI helpers (for example explaining a word or checking a sentence you write) run on Google's on-device model (Gemini Nano, through Android AICore). The text stays on your phone.

Account and AI features (optional)

You can sign in with your email address (or Google, when offered) to use AI translation of your own lyrics and "My podcast", a short spoken episode about lines you choose. Then:

  • The lines you send (and, for My podcast, their meanings) go over an encrypted connection to our server in the European Union (Amazon Web Services, Ireland). They are translated or turned into an episode by an AI model on Amazon Bedrock, and an episode is voiced by Amazon Polly, in the same region. They are not stored and not logged, and Amazon does not use them to train models. The results come back to your phone and are kept only there.
  • Your account (email address and an internal account number) is kept by Amazon Cognito. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address.
  • We keep counters of the AI songs and podcast minutes you used this month, to apply the monthly allowance. They expire on their own after about two months.
  • Delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account: the account and its counters are deleted from the server; your cards and progress stay on your phone.

Notifications

The daily reminder is optional, created on your phone and can be turned off in Settings at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. Deleting a song in "My lyrics" deletes its cards and progress immediately. If you created an account, delete it in Settings → Account → Delete account (uninstalling does not delete it), or write to the address below.
